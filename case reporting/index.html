Before you send
Authorized case staff can read your report. If software flags a possible repeat pattern, an authorized committee may see limited details such as report reference, category, and date - not your story or contact details. Similarity suggestions are leads for human review, not findings. Your report is not fully anonymous if your story identifies you. In rare situations, staff may need to share information for an immediate safety risk or legal obligation. You can ask a campus support person about privacy before deciding.
